- **CONFIRMED FACT** Hot Yoga Pilawa is an active sole proprietorship owned by Katarzyna Rattelmeier. The CEIDG extract reflected an active business as of 23 June 2026, started on 27 May 2025, with primary PKD 93.13.Z (fitness clubs).
