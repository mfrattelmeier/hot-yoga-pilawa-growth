### B. Inferno Hot Pilates operational sequence used for Kamila’s training

The studio-supplied July 2026 training script supports this class structure:

1. Welcome and verbal engagement.
2. Grounding, breath, body awareness, and neutral pelvis setup.
3. Bridge series: classic bridge, wide-leg bridge, and butterfly bridge; HYP uses 10 reps initially with targets of 20, plus 40 pulses with targets of 50–100.
4. ABS Butterflies: 6–8 Tabata rounds, 20 seconds work / 10 seconds rest, with three modifications.
5. Side plank: 45–60 seconds, with lower-knee modification.
6. Superman: 45 seconds.
7. Forearm plank: 60 seconds; Superman + forearm plank repeated three times and ending with Superman.
8. Second side plank, then Bicycle Twists: 6–8 Tabata rounds at 20/10.
9. One longer water break between floor and standing series while the instructor demonstrates the next exercise.
10. Standing series: Frog Squat (about 6 rounds, preferably pulses with miniband above knees), push-ups (about 5–6 Tabata rounds), and Mountain Climbers (about 6 rounds, with a standing modification in HYP).
11. Active Savasana with no more than 2–3 stretches, relaxation, and closing message.

**IMPLEMENTED:** A formatted version and PDF were produced.  
**UNKNOWN / NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Whether this exact sequence was formally approved as the studio standard, taught by Kamila, or later revised.

### C. Historical Brighton reference sequence

The attached table is explicitly dated **31 January 2020** and titled **“Brighton Sequence.”** It is reference material, not proof of HYP’s 2020 operations. It sets out a 60-minute class:

- 0–5 min: grounding (breath, spinal articulation, Roll the Marble), 3–5 repetitions.
- 5–12 min: bridge series—hip width, wide legged, pigeon toe’ed; each with 20 full-range repetitions, 20 calf raises, and 30 pulses.
- 12–16 min: Butterflies, 20/10, 8 rounds.
- 20–28 min: plank series—side plank, Superman variation / forearm plank, other side.
- 28–32 min: leg lifts, 20/10, 8 rounds.
- Water break.
- 32–51 min: standing/cardio—3 minutes explanation and 16 minutes work; frog squats, wide-leg burpees, triceps push-ups, mountain climbers, each 20/10 for 8 rounds.
- 51–55 min: active recovery—clams, clams variation, Leprechaun, 30 seconds each, completing one side first.
- 55–60 min: stretch/Savasana.
