## Evidence method and labels

Direct statements from the studio owner/user and legible facts in screenshots are treated as the strongest evidence. Text drafted or recommended by the AI is not treated as proof that the business adopted or implemented it. A request to draft copy is evidence that the topic was being considered or prepared, but not necessarily that the copy was published.

- **CONFIRMED FACT** — clearly stated as true/current in a chat or directly visible in a dated screenshot.
- **HISTORICAL FACT** — stated or visible as true at a specific time; it may no longer be current.
- **DECISION MADE** — the owner explicitly chose a course of action.
- **IMPLEMENTED** — the chats show that the action was carried out, not just planned.
- **PLANNED** — intended or being prepared, with no proof of completion.
- **IDEA / BRAINSTORM** — suggested or discussed without approval.
- **ASSUMPTION** — implied or supplied by the AI, but not proven by owner evidence.
- **CONTRADICTION** — two versions conflict or a later statement changes an earlier one.
- **UNKNOWN / NEEDS VERIFICATION** — important but not resolvable from the chats.
- **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED** — an explicit choice remains open.

## 1. Executive Summary

The source area depicts Hot Yoga Pilawa as an operating boutique movement studio in Pilawa with a broad weekly schedule and an established client community. The strongest evidence is operational: a dated Fitssey schedule, named instructors, class formats, booking availability, and detailed owner-authored rules. The business is not merely conceptual; classes were running through Fitssey, multiple instructors appeared in the schedule, and the studio maintained a website/public booking interface.

The most important historical development was a February 2026 tightening and simplification of commercial and booking rules. **DECISION MADE:** the studio moved toward clients managing their own bookings and cancellations in Fitssey, temporarily suspended the OPEN pass, discontinued course-type passes for a trial month, introduced longer-duration and Premium passes, and announced more consistent enforcement of the regulations. The stated reason was repeated misuse: late cancellations, no-shows, failure to cancel, requests to recover entries after a pass expired, and OPEN-pass holders reserving as many as five classes per week but attending only two. These behaviors created false sell-outs, blocked available mats, forced manual work on the owner, and reduced studio revenue.

The studio's offer in the 23 February–1 March 2026 schedule included Bikram Yoga (26&2), Hot Yoga, Hot Pilates HIIT, Hatha Yoga, Iyengar Yoga, Fascial/Yin Yoga, Healthy Spine, Mobility & Stretch, and Yoga for Children. **HISTORICAL FACT:** the schedule covered mornings, weekday late afternoons/evenings, and Saturday morning. Several evening classes showed zero or very few free places; some morning classes had substantial availability. One Mobility & Stretch class was cancelled with all 15 places shown free.

Pricing evidence is narrower than the breadth of the offer. **HISTORICAL FACT:** around January 2026, the owner stated PLN 65 for a single entry, PLN 500 for 12 entries, and PLN 450 for OPEN. **DECISION MADE:** OPEN was later suspended for February. The owner also stated that the 8-entry pass rose by PLN 2.50 per class and the 4-entry pass by PLN 5 per class, but the final total prices were not stated. Prices of PLN 240/400/460 for 4/8/10 entries were AI recommendations only and are not confirmed prices.

The studio is exploring growth beyond routine group classes. **PLANNED:** an application for an ARiMR/LGD beneficiary number and a 2026 development grant of up to PLN 200,000. **IDEA / BRAINSTORM:** development into a local wellbeing and recovery centre, a second service room, Pilates reformers, infrared/red-light services, cold plunge, senior programming, workshops, local partnerships, branded event equipment, retail, digital products, and possibly a second location or franchise. A community yoga retreat near Garwolin/Wilga was also being planned, but no venue, price, or booking was confirmed.

The principal unresolved issues are the exact current price list, whether OPEN was suspended temporarily or eliminated, the final cancellation deadline, minimum attendance rule, actual class capacity, whether the February process changes succeeded, grant eligibility and obligations, CRM ownership, lead-handling processes, and reliable financial/marketing metrics. HubSpot, Meta campaign performance, GA4, Google Ads, lead conversion, retention, and lifetime value were not evidenced in this source area.
