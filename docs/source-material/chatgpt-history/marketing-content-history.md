## Evidence method and source register

This document separates statements made by the user from content proposed by an assistant. A user statement about an existing condition or completed action is treated as stronger evidence than assistant-generated copy, recommendations, calculations, or platform instructions. An assistant draft remains an **IDEA / BRAINSTORM** unless the user explicitly adopted it, restated it as final, or confirmed that it was published or carried out. Marketing and health-benefit claims are preserved as historical messaging, not independently validated claims.

Evidence labels used throughout:

- **CONFIRMED FACT** — clearly stated as true/current in the source conversation.
- **HISTORICAL FACT** — stated as true at a particular time, but current status is not established.
- **DECISION MADE** — the user explicitly chose or finalized an option.
- **IMPLEMENTED** — the user reported that an action occurred or material was published/used.
- **PLANNED** — intended, prepared, or scheduled, but completion is not proved.
- **IDEA / BRAINSTORM** — suggested or explored without clear approval or implementation.
- **ASSUMPTION** — implied but not proved.
- **CONTRADICTION** — differing versions appear in the conversations.
- **UNKNOWN / NEEDS VERIFICATION** — important and unresolved from these chats.
- **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED** — an explicit business choice remains open.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **CONFIRMED FACT — Studio and market.** The business is consistently called **Hot Yoga Pilawa** or, in some older Polish phrasing, **Hot Joga Pilawa**. Its stated address is **ul. Dworcowa 3D, lokal LU 4, 08-440 Pilawa**. The owner described opening in a town of roughly 5,000 residents within a municipality of roughly 11,000. [S6, S7, S13]
- **HISTORICAL FACT — Strong launch.** The owner reported that groups were full before the studio opened, allowing fixed studio costs to be covered, the owner to be paid for teaching from the beginning, and the initial investment to begin paying back. She attributed the launch in part to a three-month Grow and Flow mentoring program with Bartłomiej Białkowski/Białkowski Media. [S7]
- **CONFIRMED FACT — Course-led commercial model.** The clearest commercial insight is that structured courses were considered more profitable than simply offering a full schedule of drop-in classes. Courses were used to fill groups, teach the Bikram method safely, build confidence, and move clients toward regular classes and ultimately a monthly unlimited subscription. [S1]
- **HISTORICAL FACT — Beginner-course scale.** In August 2025, Course A ran in three groups of ten women at different times of day. The Tuesday/Thursday evening group was fullest, Monday/Wednesday evening was second, and Monday/Wednesday morning had the fewest participants. [S1]
- **IMPLEMENTED — Curriculum iteration.** The beginner course was not merely brainstormed: the chats record actual delivery and post-class feedback for Level 1 classes 1–7 and Level 2 classes 1–4. The curriculum blended Bikram positions, breathing, relaxation, core/glute strength, Yin yoga, and introductory Inferno Hot Pilates elements. Materials for instructors and students, homework, attendance sheets, WhatsApp follow-up, and handout/ebook concepts were developed around it. [S1]
- **CONFIRMED FACT — Broad offer.** The source area supports regular or planned offerings across Bikram-inspired hot yoga, Inferno Hot Pilates, Vinyasa, Yin/fascial yoga, Yoga for Mature Adults/Healthy Spine, children’s yoga, beginner courses, sound baths, themed workshops, birthday sessions, attendance challenges, passes, and potential retreats. [S1–S13]
- **HISTORICAL FACT — Marketing is campaign- and content-intensive.** The studio repeatedly developed Facebook/Instagram copy, graphics, Meta campaigns, lead forms, message templates, Fitssey descriptions/newsletters, WhatsApp messages, review requests, website descriptions, and event scripts. The dominant voice is warm, personal, encouraging, local, community-led, feminine when appropriate, and premium rather than discount-led. [S1, S3, S5, S6, S8, S10–S13]
- **IMPLEMENTED — Published event pricing.** For the Karmazynowy Koń event on 22 February 2026, the user explicitly said she had published **259 zł for outside participants** and **189 zł for regular studio clients**. A 149 zł team price and a second-ticket discount were discussed, but their implementation is less certain. [S10]
- **DECISION MADE — Key promotions.** Concrete pass decisions included: 36 visits/three months at 1,200 zł instead of 1,500 zł; 24 visits/three months at 899 zł instead of 1,140 zł; and HOT SUMMER PASS, 18 visits for 699 zł through the end of August 2026. Each rewarded commitment and regular attendance. [S3, S11, S13]
- **CONFIRMED FACT — Seasonal risk.** A summer attendance decline was explicitly observed on 10 July 2026. The response was a limited seven-week/through-August summer pass intended to sustain two-to-three visits per week. [S13]
