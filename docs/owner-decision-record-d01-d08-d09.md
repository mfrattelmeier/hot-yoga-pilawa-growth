## 7. Approved Beginner Entry Route

### Preferred First Step

When a structured KURS JOGI OD PODSTAW is available, it is the owner’s preferred onboarding route because it provides a plan, fixed commitment, motivation, and a better chance of building a routine. It is not currently running and will not be mandatory.

In the current timetable, Bikram Joga (26&2) is an owner-preferred first option for a beginner who tolerates heat, accepts a 90-minute session, and has no condition requiring prior individual guidance.

### Other Approved Beginner Options

The strongest recommended options are:

- Hatha Joga dla początkujących.
- Joga powięziowa (Yin) REGENERACJA.
- Zdrowy kręgosłup/ Delikatna praktyka jogi.
- Hot Pilates (START).
- Stretching (głębokie rozciąganie).
- Iyengar Joga, despite its technical and prop-based nature, because it is taught gently.

Other current classes may be adapted for a beginner after appropriate guidance. Pilates is an important alternative for people who want strengthening work or are uncomfortable beginning with yoga for personal or religious reasons.

### Classes Not Recommended as First Step

- The advanced Inferno Hot Pilates (HIIT) group on Monday/Wednesday 18:00 and Saturday 08:30.

### When the Beginner Course Is Preferred

- For a person who wants structure, progression, fixed dates, and external motivation.
- For customers likely to struggle to choose appropriate classes or build consistency in an open timetable.
- The course should be relaunched only after schedule, program, price, and group recruitment are approved.

### When a Normal Class Is Appropriate

- A beginner may start directly in the open timetable; completing a course is not required.
- The chosen class should reflect the customer’s goals, preferred intensity, confidence, and heat tolerance.
- Instructors provide modifications, and the customer is encouraged to work at an individual pace.

### Situations Requiring Individual Guidance

A customer should contact the studio before choosing a class when pregnant, returning after an injury or accident, living with a chronic condition, taking medication that may affect heat/exercise tolerance, experiencing significant mobility limitations, uncertain about heat tolerance, or anxious/unsure about the first class. The studio selects a suitable route individually and does not diagnose or replace professional medical advice.

## 8. Approved Beginner Messaging

### APPROVED

- “No previous experience is required.”
- “You do not need to be flexible.”
- “You can practise at your own pace.”
- “The instructor will offer modifications and help you adapt the practice.”
- “Do not compare yourself with other people; listen to your own body.”

- “Most classes can accommodate beginners.” State clearly that the advanced Inferno Hot Pilates group is not a first-class recommendation.
- “Bikram Yoga can be an excellent first choice.” Use only with the heat-tolerance, 90-minute-session, and individual-guidance conditions above.

- Katarzyna’s long teaching history and work with thousands of participants may be used as expertise-based positioning, provided exact tenure or numbers are not made more specific than the evidence supports.
- Personal and customer stories may describe individual experiences after injury, but must not be rewritten as guaranteed treatment outcomes.
- Katarzyna’s personal experience of practising during pregnancy may be told only as an individual story, not general suitability advice. Pregnant customers require professional and studio consultation before participation.

Suggested positive positioning:

> Hot Yoga Pilawa specializes in Bikram Yoga (26&2), taught in a heated studio by Katarzyna, an instructor with extensive experience gained across different schools and with thousands of participants. Katarzyna’s experience and customer reports indicate that regular heated practice may support relaxation, freedom of movement, mobility, recovery, and general wellbeing. For many people it has been an important part of returning to regular movement after injury or a long break.

Suggested safety line:

> If you are pregnant, returning after an injury or accident, have a chronic condition, take medication that may affect exercise or heat tolerance, or are unsure how you respond to heat, consult an appropriate healthcare professional and contact the studio before your first class.

### NOT APPROVED

- “All classes are suitable for beginners.”
- “Hot yoga is suitable for everyone.”
- Claims that heat guarantees injury prevention, heals injuries or disease, or produces results superior to physiotherapy.
- Guaranteed medical or therapeutic outcomes.

These exclusions are internal copy guardrails; customer-facing marketing does not need to lead with a list of negative disclaimers.
