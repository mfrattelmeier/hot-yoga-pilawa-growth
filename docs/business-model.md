## Executive Summary

Hot Yoga Pilawa is an operating boutique yoga and movement studio in Pilawa. The current technical audit confirms substantial Fitssey operating history, a paid-social lead base in HubSpot, and active Meta advertising assets. Historical evidence shows a model built around group classes, structured beginner pathways, passes, courses, workshops/events, and a warm local community. It also records ambitions in recovery, strength/longevity, hybrid delivery, retreats, and corporate/BUR services; these are not assumed to be live. [Sources: audits/fitssey/current-state.md; audits/hubspot/current-state.md; audits/meta/current-state.md; marketing-content-history.md; general-unprojected-chats-history.md]

## Customer Journey

Historical evidence supports a practical journey of discovery or inquiry → human follow-up or self-service booking → Fitssey registration/booking → first visit → pass/course/event purchase → repeat attendance → renewal or lapse → reactivation/referral. Structured beginner courses historically reduced confidence barriers and prepared some participants for regular hot classes. The current audit confirms that these stages are not represented as a unified lifecycle in HubSpot. [Sources: marketing-content-history.md; general-unprojected-chats-history.md; audits/cross-system-gap-analysis.md]
